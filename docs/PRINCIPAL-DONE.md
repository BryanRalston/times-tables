# Principal-done bar

A Loudoun Grade 3 teacher could recommend this for 2026–27. Not a demo.

## Must be true

1. Home | Lessons lists every cell in `docs/LCPS-2026-27-Grade-3-Mathematics-Yearly-Overview.pdf`.
2. Every 2023 SOL 3.NS.1–4, 3.CE.1–2, 3.MG.1–4, 3.PS.1, 3.PFA.1 has a **visible model board**, not a title over a keypad.
3. **Data cycle (3.PS.1):** the child tallies or sorts, then reads the graph they made — `u1-tally`, `u6-picto`, `u7-bar`.
4. **Word form (3.NS.1.a):** six-digit numbers in words ↔ digits on a place chart — `u2-word`.
5. **Measurement (3.MG.1):** length, weight/mass, liquid volume; U.S. Customary and metric; nearest half or whole; ruler/scale/beaker with numbered ticks and a pointer — `u8-length`, `u8-mass`, `u8-volume`.
6. **Money (3.NS.4):** count, compare, **make this amount** with bills and coins up to $5, leftover/change — `u11-make`.
7. Graphs actually draw. Leftover dots are optional. Replay a lesson → different items, still correct. Squishee shelf + poke.
8. A third grader can: open Home, pick a lesson, type an answer they can see, Check, go back Home.
9. **× as equal jumps** has its own board — `u3-jumps`.
10. Unit and lesson titles follow the UI language (English / Spanish / Brazilian Portuguese).
11. Scored answers are audited: 25 generated items per activity, choices include the key, models match the math.
