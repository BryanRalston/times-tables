# Academy language

Squishee Academy starts in English. The hello screen shows English, Español, and Português, and the same choice is in Settings and on the Grown-ups page. The choice is saved on this device (`save.lang`, `save.langChosen`).

The Spanish is Latin American and kid-friendly, with tú. The Portuguese is Brazilian and kid-friendly, with você. Character names (Peach, Frog, and the bosses) stay as they are. Squishee stays Squishee. Portuguese strings and the Portuguese voice map load only after Português is chosen, so an English first visit stays the same size.

## English reading games

Phonics, Sight Words, and Spelling teach English reading. The word, the sentence, and the letter tiles stay in English. The instruction around them is translated: Spanish “Toca la palabra que escuchas” and Portuguese “Toque a palavra que você ouve”. The recorded voice for that language says the instruction and also says the English word, so one offline voice can coach both.

## Stories

Read-along books are English only for now. Español and Português hide the Stories card and the book text. A grown-up report can say how many books were finished, and it does not print the English pages.

## Worksheets

The in-app printable follows the language the child is using. The public pages that search engines read stay in English, so a shared link does not change language with the browser.

## Voice

Spanish speech uses Kokoro `ef_dora`. Portuguese speech uses Kokoro `pf_dora` with `lang="pt-br"`. Clips load only after that language is chosen. English players do not download them. Details are in `docs/VOICE.md`.
