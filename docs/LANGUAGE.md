# Academy language

Squishee Academy starts in English. The hello screen shows English and Español, and the same choice is in Settings and on the Grown-ups page. The choice is saved on this device (`save.lang`, `save.langChosen`).

The Spanish is Latin American and kid-friendly, with tú. Character names (Peach, Frog, and the bosses) stay as they are. Squishee stays Squishee.

## English reading games

Phonics, Sight Words, and Spelling teach English reading. The word, the sentence, and the letter tiles stay in English. The instruction around them is Spanish: “Toca la palabra que escuchas”, “Deletrea la palabra que escuchas”, “¿Qué dibujo empieza con mmm?”. The recorded Spanish voice says those instructions and also says the English word, so one offline voice can coach both.

## Stories

Read-along books are English only for now. Español hides the Stories card and the book text. A grown-up report in Spanish can say how many books were finished, and it does not print the English pages. Spanish books need their own `ef_dora` clips before they can show.

## Worksheets

The in-app printable follows the language the child is using. The public pages that search engines read stay in English, so a shared link does not change language with the browser.

## Voice

Spanish speech uses Kokoro `ef_dora`. Clips load only after Español is chosen. English players do not download them. Details are in `docs/VOICE.md`.
